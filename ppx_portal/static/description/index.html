Payment Portal Express

Let your customers pay their invoices online, by card or bank transfer

Adds a Pay via Portal button to customer invoices and to customers. One click opens that invoice in your Payment Portal Express account, where your customer pays without needing an Odoo login or a password.

Paid invoices close themselves

The payment is written back to Odoo against the invoice, reconciled through your bank journal's Outstanding Receipts account. Nobody keys it in twice, and the invoice stops chasing the customer the moment it is paid.

Card or bank transfer

Customers can pay by card, or by bank account where your processor supports it. If you charge a convenience fee on card payments, it is raised as its own invoice line using the Credit Card Fee product, so your ledger ties out.

Card details never touch Odoo

Card entry happens on the payment processor's own hosted fields, and only a reference comes back. No card number, security code or bank account number passes through this module, your Odoo database or our servers.

Works on Odoo Online

This is a data module: no custom Python. It installs on Odoo Online, Odoo.sh and on-premise alike.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module.
  2. Open Settings > Payment Portal Express > Settings and enter your account name, exactly as it appears on the Accounts page of your portal admin.
  3. Run Settings > Payment Portal Express > Finish setup once. It sets the Outstanding Receipts account on your bank journals and creates a place for saved cards to live.
  4. Open a posted customer invoice and click Pay via Portal.

A Payment Portal Express account is required. See paymentportalexpress.com.

What it sends us

When someone clicks the button, the link carries the invoice's Odoo portal access token. Our portal uses it to read that customer's open invoices from your Odoo, so it can show what is owed and write the payment back. Nothing else is collected, and nothing is sent anywhere until the button is clicked.
